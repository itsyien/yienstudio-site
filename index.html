Yien Studio

Independent games, creative apps, and technology experiments built by Yien.

Games

  • CitixChase — One city. Two drivers. The Runner escapes through relays — the Chaser has five minutes to stop them.
  • Yien Royale 2 — A fast low-poly battle royale with readable FPS combat and high-stakes drops.
  • Lava Parkour — Climb above rising lava, manage jetpack fuel, and dodge fireballs.
  • Undead Rush — Survive endless zombie waves, earn credits, and build your loadout mid-run.
  • Yien Royale Legacy — Classic arena FPS — grab a spawn weapon, drop in, and fight bots.

Apps

  • The Void: Satisfying file tool — Drag files into an animated black hole to recycle, archive, copy, or ZIP — a standalone Windows tool with history and undo.
  • Internet Planetarium — Transforms every new tab into an interactive universe of the websites that matter to you — planets, galaxies, and fast search.
  • PureFlow Launcher — An Android launcher built for a clean, modern, minimal home screen — glass folders, soft wallpapers, and fluid layout.

Experimental

  • Latch — A no-admin Windows utility that gives completed downloads a safe lifetime the moment they arrive — ignore the prompt and nothing changes.
