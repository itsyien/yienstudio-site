PureFlow Launcher

PureFlow Launcher is an Android personalization app that replaces your default home screen with a calmer, more modern desktop. Think glassmorphic folders, soft pastel wallpapers, and floating icons with room to breathe — designed so your phone feels clean instead of cluttered. Pick wallpapers, tune glass opacity, organize apps into folders, and keep everything readable over bright or colorful backgrounds.

Platform: Android

How to use

  • Install PureFlow and set it as your default home / launcher app.
  • Choose a wallpaper — system wallpaper, solid color, soft mesh fades, or your own image.
  • Arrange apps on the fluid workspace; drag into glass folders to stay organized.
  • Open settings to adjust glass UI, icon style, labels, and gesture options.

Features

  • Glass UI folders and icons with adjustable opacity
  • Custom wallpapers, solid colors, and soft mesh-fade palettes
  • Floating workspace with folders and app drawer search
  • Icon customization (crop / edit) and optional frosted acrylic look
  • Gesture navigation support and in-app tutorial
